## 3. Description of the Service

Expense Tracker helps you manually record and review personal financial activity, including income, expenses, accounts, transfers, budgets, categories, and reports. The App is designed to work **offline** for core features and stores your records **locally on your device**.

### 3.1 What the App includes

Depending on your access level, the App may allow you to:

- Record income and expenses with notes, categories, accounts, and dates
- View monthly summaries, balances, and spending insights
- Manage cash, bank, card, and wallet accounts
- Record same-currency and cross-currency transfers
- Set monthly budgets and receive optional on-device budget alerts (Premium)
- Manage custom categories (Premium)
- Use advanced statistics, filters, and reporting (Premium)
- Export your data as JSON or CSV
- Enable optional biometric or device-credential app lock
- Purchase or restore a Premium subscription

### 3.2 What the App does not provide

The current version of the App does **not** provide:

- Bank account linking, payment processing, or automatic transaction import
- Receipt scanning or photo-based data capture
- Cloud sync, cloud backup, or account recovery after uninstall
- Data import from other apps or files
- Live exchange-rate lookup or guaranteed currency conversion rates
- Professional financial, tax, investment, accounting, or legal advice
- A guarantee that records are complete, accurate, or suitable for official filings

You are solely responsible for verifying your entries, exports, and any decisions you make based on information shown in the App.
